# 🏦 Toss (DESIGN.md Reference)

> "강렬한 신뢰와 극강의 가미니멀리즘이 조화된 핀테크 표준"

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
- **Core Concept**: Calm, Confident, and Deceptively Simple.
- **Mood**: Precise · Bright · Reliable · Optimized
- **Density**: 5/10 — Mobile-first optimization with clear hierarchy.
- **Backgrounds**: Pure White (#ffffff), Light Grey (#f2f4f6).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Toss Blue** (#3182f6): Primary interactive color. CTAs, links, active states.
- **Pure White** (#ffffff): Page background, card surfaces.
- **Dark Charcoal** (#191f28): Primary heading color (near-black with blue undertone).
- **Grey 600** (#6b7684): Body text, descriptions, metadata.
- **Error Red** (#f04452): Negative indicators, destructive actions.
- **Success Green** (#03b26c): Positive financial indicators.

---

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Font Family**: 
  - **Preferred (KR)**: `Pretendard, "Noto Sans KR", -apple-system, sans-serif`
  - **Standard**: `"Toss Product Sans", "Tossface", "SF Pro Display", Roboto, sans-serif`
- **Hierarchy**:
  - Display Hero: 30px, weight 700, 1.33 line-height.
  - Heading Large: 22px, weight 700.
  - Body (Reading): 14px, weight 400, 1.57 line-height.
- **OpenType**: Supports Tabular Numerals (tnum) for financial data.

---

## 4. Layout Principles
- **Grid**: Mobile-first baseline 375px. 20px horizontal padding.
- **Spacing**: Base unit 8px.
- **Shadows**: Minimal elevation. `0px 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`.
- **Radius**: 12px (Standard Card), 8px (Inputs/Buttons).

---

## 5. Component Stylings
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: Toss Blue (#3182f6) background, white text, 12px radius.
  - Secondary: Blue Light (#e8f3ff) background, Toss Blue text.
- **Cards**: Border-radius 12px, background #ffffff, minimal shadow.
- **Inputs**: Background #f2f4f6, 8px radius, focus border 2px Toss Blue.

---

## 6. Do & Don't
- ✅ **Do**: 금융 숫자에는 고정폭 숫자(Tabular Numerals)를 사용하십시오.
- ✅ **Do**: 인터랙티브 요소는 반드시 Toss Blue (#3182f6)를 사용하십시오.
- 🚫 **Don't**: 브랜드 블루(#0064FF, 로고용)를 UI 버튼에 사용하지 마십시오.
- 🚫 **Don't**: 과도한 그림자나 깊이감을 사용하지 마십시오 (투명한 레이어링 우선).
