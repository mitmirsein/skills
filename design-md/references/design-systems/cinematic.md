# 🎥 Cinematic Overview (DESIGN.md Reference)

> "A living documentary. Empty darkness filled with subtle motion and breathing visuals."

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
- **Core Concept**: Split-screen documentary style. Deep darkness on the left, cinematic AI artwork shining through on the right.
- **Mood**: Profound · Cinematic · Floating · Narrative
- **Layout Type**: cinematic

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Deep Void Black** (#030406): Absolute dark background for the text panel (bg_start, bg_mid, bg_end).
- **Primary White** (#fdfdfd): Main title text.
- **Silver Smoke** (#a0a4ab): Reading body text.
- **Muted Gray** (#6d727b): Secondary translation text.
- **Gold Accent** (#d4af37): Very subtle elegant highlights (Accent).
- **Subtle Line** (#1a1b1e): Outline pattern (Pattern).

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Font Family**: `Pretendard, -apple-system, sans-serif`
- Emphasis on large, confident, thick typography on the left panel.

## 4. Layout Principles (Cinematic Rule)
- The layout MUST allow half of the screen (the right side) to be entirely transparent, so the background Ken Burns video layer can show through.
- The left uses a dark solid overlay mask `#030406` with a slight soft gradient masking the center.
