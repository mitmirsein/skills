# 💳 Stripe (DESIGN.md Reference)

> "기술적 정교함과 럭셔리한 정밀함의 정점"

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
- **Core Concept**: Technical Luxury.
- **Mood**: Sophisticated · Precise · Atmospheric · Confident
- **Density**: 6/10 — Balanced density with engineered whitespace.
- **Backgrounds**: Pure White (#ffffff), Brand Dark (#1c1e54).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Stripe Purple** (#533afd): Primary brand color, CTAs, interactive highlights.
- **Deep Navy** (#061b31): Primary heading color (warm near-black blue).
- **Slate** (#64748d): Body text, descriptions.
- **Ruby** (#ea2261) / **Magenta** (#f96bee): Decorative accents and gradients.
- **Success Green** (#15be53): Status indicators.

---

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Font Family**: 
  - **Preferred (KR)**: `Pretendard, "Noto Sans KR", -apple-system, sans-serif`
  - **Standard**: `sohne-var`, "SF Pro Display", sans-serif
- **Hierarchy**:
  - Display Hero: 56px, weight 300 (Extra Light), tight line-height (1.03), tracking -1.4px.
  - Heading Large: 48px, weight 300.
  - Body: 16px, weight 300-400, 1.4 line-height.
- **OpenType**: ss01 (Stylistic Set 1) enabled globally. tnum (Tabular) for data.

---

## 4. Layout Principles
- **Containment**: Max content width ~1080px.
- **Section Rhythm**: Alternating white and dark brand sections (#1c1e54).
- **Shadows**: Multi-layered & Blue-tinted. `rgba(50,50,93,0.25)`.
- **Radius**: Conservative 4px (Standard) ~ 8px (Featured).

---

## 5. Component Stylings
- **Buttons**:
  - Primary: Stripe Purple (#533afd) background, white text, 4px radius.
  - Ghost: Transparent, 1px solid #b9b9f9, Purple text.
- **Cards**: White background, 1px solid #e5edf5 border, multi-layer shadow.
- **Badges**: 4px radius, success variant use rgba(21,190,83,0.2) bg with solid green text.

---

## 6. Do & Don't
- ✅ **Do**: 헤드라인에는 Weight 300(가벼운 굵기)을 사용하여 권위감을 표현하십시오.
- ✅ **Do**: 그림자에는 반드시 푸른색조(Blue-tint)를 섞어 브랜드 색감을 유지하십시오.
- 🚫 **Don't**: 굵은(Bold) 헤드라인 지양 (Stripe는 가벼운 굵기에서의 정교함을 지향함).
- 🚫 **Don't**: 과도한 라운딩(Pill shape) 금지. 보수적인 4-8px 범위를 유지하십시오.
